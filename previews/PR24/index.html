BlackboxComrade.MaternSlopePS — Type
MaternSlopePS(ℓ, α)

The Matérn power spectrum S(k) ∝ (1 + ℓ² k²)^(-α/2) written by its outer scale ℓ (in pixels) and its high-k slope α: MaternPS(ρ, ν) with α = 2(ν + 1) and ℓ = ρ / √(8ν). α = 2 gives equal power per log k below the outer scale, the limit ν → 0 that the (ρ, ν) form reaches only at its edge.

BlackboxComrade._field_scale_moves — Method
_field_scale_moves(post, view, θ, spec)

"field_scale" moves: trade each non-centered sky field X against its scale σX: the latent coordinate of σX moves by u and each compensated coefficient X_k → X_k · σX / σX′, with log|det ∂x′/∂x| = n_c log(σX / σX′) for n_c compensated coefficients.

With spec.band_limit = Inf every coefficient is compensated; the image depends on σX .* X only, so the likelihood is unchanged and the move is accepted on the prior alone. A finite band_limit (stationary random fields only) compensates only the coefficients with wavenumber up to band_limit times the longest baseline. The rest keep their values, so the image changes at those wavenumbers and the move is accepted on the full posterior; in exchange the scale's conditional width given the coefficients is near 1/√(2n_c) instead of 1/√(2n). The default initial scale is 2.4 / √(2n_c).

source
BlackboxComrade._mean_field_moves — Method
_mean_field_moves(post, view, θ, spec)

"mean_field" moves: trade one mean-model parameter against the log-intensity field a of the PolExp stationary random-field sky model: the parameter's latent coordinate moves by u, changing the mean image m → m′, and the white coefficients of a move by the inverse of the linear map from coefficients to σa · δa, applied to log m − log m′. Then m′ exp(σa δa′) = m exp(σa δa) pixelwise and Stokes I, Q, U and V, the flux normalization and the centering are all unchanged. The coefficient shift depends on the other coordinates only, so log|det ∂x′/∂x| = 0.

BlackboxComrade._rho_field_moves — Method
_rho_field_moves(post, view, θ, spec)

"rho_field" moves: trade one spectral parameter ρₙ of a stationary random field X (a Markov RF correlation length, or the Matérn outer scale ℓ = ρ₁ or slope α = ρ₂) against its white coefficients: the latent coordinate of ρₙ moves by u and each compensated coefficient is multiplied by A_k(ρ) rtnrm(ρ) / (A_k(ρ′) rtnrm(ρ′)), the ratio of the factors genfield applies to it, with log|det ∂x′/∂x| = Σ_k log(A_k(ρ) rtnrm(ρ)) − log(A_k(ρ′) rtnrm(ρ′)) over the compensated coefficients. Each term of each field is its own move.

With spec.band_limit = Inf every coefficient is compensated, so the field and the likelihood are unchanged and the move is accepted on the prior alone. A finite band_limit compensates only the coefficients with wavenumber up to band_limit times the longest baseline and is accepted on the full posterior, as for "field_scale".

BlackboxComrade.add_coherency_noise — Function
add_coherency_noise(data, ferr, ferr_leak = 0.0)

A copy of data with fractional noise added in quadrature to each measurement.

For coherency matrices, each element Vᵢⱼ (station a's feed i, station b's feed j) gets

σᵢⱼ² → σᵢⱼ² + ferr² |Vᵢⱼ|² + ferr_leak² (|V_{īj}|² + |V_{ij̄}|²) / 2,

where ī and j̄ are the other feed of each station. The ferr term is the error from fractional gain errors on the feeds (residual gains, bandpass, delays). The ferr_leak term is the error from leakage errors on the feeds, which move the other feed's signal into Vᵢⱼ: station a's leakage brings in V{īj} and station b's brings in V{ij̄}. Both fractions are per baseline, so on a circular-feed baseline ferr = ferr_leak = f adds f |I| to every element, up to terms in V. Both terms hold in the feed frame of the data (no field-rotation correction applied) for any feed basis, and for circular feeds in any frame. Entries missing from the data (NaN, e.g. the absent feed of a single-feed station) contribute nothing and keep their noise.

Other data products have no feeds; they get Comrade's add_fractional_noise(data, ferr), and ferr_leak must be zero.

BlackboxComrade.build_fitting_config — Method
build_fitting_config(cfg::AbstractDict) -> FittingStrategy

Parse a fitting-strategy TOML into a FittingStrategy. Sections: [optimizer], [tempering], [sampler], [run]. sampler.algorithm is "nuts" (default), whose backend (AdvancedHMC NUTS vs Reactant NUTS with its diagonal metric) follows run.use_reactant, or "mclmc" or "walnuts", which run sampler.chains chains of KineticSamplers MCLMC or WALNUTS (see sample_kinetic) and need run.use_reactant = true. nadapt is the warmup length per chain, in steps for MCLMC (default 150 000) and in draws for WALNUTS (default 1000), and nsample the draws kept per chain (default 1000). For MCLMC a draw is kept every thin (default 1) blocks of chunk_size steps, so sampling runs nsample * thin * chunk_size steps per chain, and max_step_halvings (default 3) is the number of halvings of a step whose energy error is too large: above max_energy_error in total (default 0.25 √d, KineticSamplers' default). integrator is MCLMC's integrator: "mclachlan" (default), "leapfrog" or "baoab" (see KineticSamplers.BAOAB). max_mode_phase is the phase per step at the 0.99 quantile of the curvature of the MCLMC warmup's step-size cap (default 1.2 with McLachlan, which keeps a harmonic mode's variance within about 1%; see sample_kinetic). max_mode_bias sets that phase from the variance bias of a harmonic mode instead, such as 0.05 for 5%, for McLachlan or leapfrog; leapfrog and BAOAB need one of the two. final (default 20% of nadapt) is the MCLMC steps per chain of the warmup's final phase, which runs after the trajectory length is set, at the capped step size and that trajectory length, so the chains settle at the sampling parameters before any draw is kept. stiff_directions (default 32) and stiff_draws (default 8) set the KineticSamplers.StiffDirections of the inverse mass of MCLMC and WALNUTS: the number of stiff directions it shrinks and the draws whose Hessians each estimate uses. For MCLMC, trajectory_rows (default "all") is the rows of the warmup's windows whose autocorrelation times set the trajectory length: "all" every latent coordinate and the projections onto the low-rank directions of the inverse mass, "projections" the projections alone. trajectory_quantile (default 1, the slowest row) is the quantile of their autocorrelation times that sets it, or "mean" for their mean, and trajectory_factor (default 1, and 0.4 with the mean) is the factor on the step size times that time, both as in KineticSamplers' PooledAdaptation. refit_trajectory_step (default 1) is the fraction of the way, on a log scale, that each window after a refit moves the trajectory length toward its estimate, and window_iterations (default 2) is the number of windows after the adaptation, each starting at the trajectory length the one before gave. checkpoint_minutes (default 60) is the wall time between the checkpoints an MCLMC or WALNUTS warmup writes, from which --restart resumes an unfinished warmup (see sample_kinetic); 0 writes none. trajectory_length, in units of √d, fixes MCLMC's trajectory length, and the warmup then runs no window; left out, the windows set it. step_size fixes MCLMC's sampling step size; left out, the warmup's adapted step size is used. bias_steps (default 2000) is the steps of the coupled runs of the MCLMC bias check after the warmup, a multiple of 250 of at least 500, or 0 to skip the check. bias_tuning (default false) reduces the warmup's MCLMC step size until a coupled bias check passes (see sample_kinetic); without it the check runs once and is logged, and bias_tuning = true is an error with step_size or with bias_steps = 0. After sampling, the run logs and writes the autocorrelation times of the scalar parameters in units of the trajectory length (see trajectory_report). For WALNUTS every draw is kept, chunk_size (default 10) is the draws per sampling block, which must divide nsample, and max_tree_depth (default 10) is the most trajectory doublings. step_size is an error with WALNUTS, the run checkpoints are errors with either algorithm, and max_tree_depth is an error with MCLMC. run.start may also name the run base of an MCLMC or WALNUTS run (the directory holding its chain_k), whose chains' last draws start the chains one to one.

run.posterior_images (default 500) is the number of posterior draws rendered to FITS images after sampling (all of them when the chains hold fewer); 0 writes none. The draws are evenly spaced through each chain, with the chains contributing equally.

run.device ("cpu" or "gpu") selects the Reactant backend and needs run.use_reactant = true. Left out, Reactant uses a GPU when one is available and the CPU otherwise. "gpu" is an error when no GPU is available.

[optimizer] fix_scales lists scalar sky parameters (e.g. ["σb", "σc", "σd"]) that every optimization stage holds at the median of their prior; the other parameters are optimized as usual. After the last stage, every non-centered sky field X with scale σX (in polexp_srf: a, b, c, d) is rescaled to σX * rms(X) and X / rms(X), which leaves the image unchanged and puts the white coefficients at the radius the prior's typical set has (rms(X) = 1); see rescale_fields. The sampler starts from that point with every parameter free. Empty (the default) changes nothing. A name that is not a scalar sky parameter of the model is an error when optimization starts.

[[sampler.moves]] tables list Metropolis–Hastings moves run between the Reactant NUTS chunks (see build_moves), each table one move kind:

[[sampler.moves]]
kind = "mean_field"
params = ["fwhm"]     # optional: a subset of the parameters the kind acts on
rounds = 10           # optional: proposals of each move per call (default 1)
target_accept = 0.45  # optional: warmup acceptance target (default 0.45)
initial_scale = 0.01  # optional: initial random-walk step scale (default per kind)
band_limit = 3.0      # optional, field_scale and rho_field only (default inf), see below

The kinds, all of which leave the likelihood unchanged unless band_limit is finite:

  • "flux_gain": total flux against a common shift of the gain log-amplitudes, lg1μ when the gain scheme has it and the lg1 chain otherwise (no params);
  • "field_scale": each non-centered sky field against its scale σX (params: fields);
  • "rho_field": each spectral parameter of each stationary random field (Markov RF correlation lengths, Matérn outer scale and slope) against the field's white coefficients (params: fields); for these two kinds a finite band_limit compensates only the white coefficients with wavenumber up to band_limit times the longest baseline (stationary random fields only); the image then changes at higher wavenumbers and the move is accepted on the full posterior;
  • "mean_field": each mean-model parameter against the log-intensity field a (PolExp stationary random-field models; params: mean-model parameters);
  • "phase_sheet": a ±2π shift of a real-line Gauss–Markov phase chain from a point on (params: chain terms; no initial_scale); "stdnormal" space only;
  • "chain_hyper": each fitted hyperparameter field (σ, τ, D) of a Gauss–Markov instrument chain against the chain's whitened innovations, with the chain values fixed; one move per term and field, which steps every site's hyperparameter and accepts each site on its own, so a round is one sweep over the sites (params: chain terms); "stdnormal" space only.

A kind may appear more than once only with disjoint params. Moves run with the Reactant NUTS, MCLMC and WALNUTS samplers (use_reactant = true), not with the CPU NUTS sampler. Unknown kinds and keys are errors, as are the list form moves = [...] and moves_per_chunk. With NUTS the moves run once per sampler chunk (chunk_size, or sample_checkpoint when set); with MCLMC on every chain once per 50 blocks of chunk_size steps, and with WALNUTS on every chain once per 10 blocks.

[run] latent_space picks the space the optimizer and sampler work in: "flat" (default, asflat) or "stdnormal" (the StdNormal transport, where the prior is exactly N(0, I); required by priors without a flat transform such as AngularProjectedNormal). In the "stdnormal" space the start point's real-line phase chains are re-wrapped to their shortest steps (see unwrap_phase_chains) before sampling.

BlackboxComrade.build_moves — Method
build_moves(post::VLBIPosterior, specs, θ0; space = nothing, output = nothing) -> Comrade.MoveSet

The moves the MoveSpecs specs describe for post in the latent space space (nothing for flat, or ProbabilityTransports.StdNormal()), as a Comrade.MoveSet, the between_chunks hook of the Reactant NUTS sampler. A spec expands to one move per field ("field_scale"), per field and spectral parameter ("rho_field"), or per mean-model parameter ("mean_field"); "flux_gain" is Comrade.flux_gain_move on sky.flux.ftot and instrument.lg1, "phase_sheet" is one Comrade.PhaseSheetMove over the real-line phase chains ((:gp1, :gprat)), and "chain_hyper" expands to one Comrade.ChainHyperMove per chain term and hyperparameter field. Each move takes its spec's rounds, target_accept and initial_scale (default per kind); output is where the MoveSet writes its statistics.

Errors if a move does not apply to the model, or if at θ0 (a constrained parameter point of post) a move that should leave the likelihood unchanged does not.

BlackboxComrade.build_sky_config — Method
build_sky_config(cfg::AbstractDict; beam=nothing) -> (SkyModel, imgdata)

Construct the SkyModel and (optional) centroid-regularizer image data from a parsed image/sky TOML. imgdata is nothing unless model.creg = true.

beam is the observation's nominal resolution in radians (beamsize(dcoh)); the driver passes it so the random-field correlation length and the mean-Gaussian width are set from the data rather than hand-tuned. The random-field correlation length defaults to model.beamsize_beams × beam (1× by default); an explicit model.beamsize (μas) overrides. When beam is nothing (building the sky standalone, without data) the legacy 20 μas / fwhm defaults are used.

model.rho_prior picks the prior family for the per-term correlation lengths of a Markov RF expansion (order < 0): "uniform" (default) or "lognormal". It applies to no other base, so setting it alongside a different order is an error.

model.center sets whether the sky model re-centers its raster on its own centroid. Without the key the choice comes from centerfix of the mean model, except under model.creg = true, which turns re-centering off. center = false turns it off explicitly (with or without creg), center = true turns it on; center = true alongside creg = true is an error, since both pin the image position.

model.center_power is the intensity power p ≥ 1 of the power_centroid that both mechanisms use, 1 (the center of light) by default. p = 2 follows the bright compact structure and barely responds to faint diffuse flux. The power reaches the model only through re-centering or the centroid regularizer, so setting it while neither is in effect is an error.

[overrides] replaces individual sky-prior entries by name (see apply_sky_overrides for the matching rules). A value is a distribution spec table ({ dist = "Normal", args = [...] }, parsed by parse_dist) for a scalar prior entry (σa, ...), or an array of one such table per term for a Markov RF correlation length (ρa, ...), whose prior is an NTuple of one distribution per order.

BlackboxComrade.feed_retarder_jones — Method
feed_retarder_jones(θ, η, δ, χ) -> (j11, j21, j12, j22)

Jones matrix, in column-major order and the circular basis, of two linear probes behind a retarder, relative to an ideal quarter-wave plate. In the linear basis, with the rotation R(a) = [cos a -sin a; sin a cos a], the retarder is Q = R(-a) P R(a) with fast axis at a = θ - π/4 and P = diag(e^{ib/2}, e^{-ib/2}) of retardance b = π/2 + η. The probes are the rows of

M = [ cos χ                         i sin χ
     -cos χ sin δ - i sin χ cos δ   cos χ cos δ - i sin χ sin δ ]

feed 1 along the first axis and feed 2 rotated by δ from orthogonal to it, each elliptical with ellipticity angle χ in the same sense: cos χ times its axis plus i sin χ times its axis turned by +π/2. A linear field E gives the voltages M Q E, so the Jones matrix is Λ⁻¹ M Q T, with T the circular-to-linear transform of Comrade's CirBasis and Λ = diag(1, i) the feed phases of the ideal plate, which the gains absorb. It is the identity at θ = η = δ = χ = 0. To first order its leakage is d1 = J₁₂/J₁₁ = -η/2 - χ + iθ and d2 = J₂₁/J₂₂ = η/2 - χ + i(θ + δ): each parameter moves one of the four real directions of the two d-terms.

A rotation of the whole receiver, plate and probes together, is not a parameter: it multiplies the Jones matrix on the right by T⁻¹ R T, which is diagonal in the circular basis, so to first order it is an R-L phase difference, which the gain ratio holds.

BlackboxComrade.markov_rho_prior — Method
markov_rho_prior(kind, grid, beamsize, order::Int; kwargs...) -> NTuple{order}

The per-term correlation-length priors of an order-order Markov random field on grid, in pixels. kind is the family marker:

  • UniformRhoPrior: every term is uniform on [lower, max(size(grid)...)].
  • LogNormalRhoPrior: term 1 is log-normal with median median_first — half the larger grid dimension, the largest structure the field can carry — and log-sd logsd_first; terms n ≥ 2 are log-normal with median median_rest, the data beam beamsize in pixels, and the tighter log-sd logsd_rest. The unconstrained coordinate of every term is log ρ. Every term is truncated to [lower, upper] pixels (default 1 pixel to the larger grid dimension): a correlation length under a pixel is not resolved by the grid and makes the white coefficients of the data-constrained modes stiff, and one longer than the grid leaves the field nearly constant across it.

BlackboxComrade.parse_init — Method
parse_init(spec, process, where_) -> AbstractInitialPrior

Parse the init entry of a kind = "gaussmarkov" site prior. spec is nothing (no init given), a string, or a table with a kind:

  • "stationary": the process's Gaussian stationary marginal (real-line processes only).
  • "uniform": uniform on (−π, π], the stationary law of a wrapped process — it lets the chain absorb the per-track phase offset, so no separate offset term is needed.
  • { kind = "fixed", value = 0.0 }: the chain is conditioned to start at value, so it describes the drift about a separately parameterized level (e.g. a gpratμ offset).
  • { kind = "gaussian", mu = 0.0, sigma = 5.0 }: an explicit N(mu, sigma²) first marginal, e.g. a deliberately diffuse start.

With no init the default is each process's own stationary law: uniform on the circle for a wrapped process, the Gaussian stationary marginal otherwise. Comrade rejects the combinations that do not exist (e.g. "stationary" for a wrapped process).

source
BlackboxComrade.parse_process — Method
parse_process(spec::AbstractDict) -> AbstractGaussMarkovProcess

Parse a TOML process spec into a Gauss-Markov process for a [GaussMarkovSitePrior], restricted to the closed allowlist:

  • kind = "OrnsteinUhlenbeck" (alias "ou"): the stationary real-line process. sigma (marginal std) and tau (correlation time in hours) are each a number (fixed) or a distribution spec (fitted hyperparameter); mu is an optional fixed number (default 0.0, not fittable).
  • kind = "BrownianMotion" (alias "bm"): the non-reverting real-line random walk. D is the diffusion coefficient (parameter² per hour), a number or a distribution spec. Nonstationary, so the prior needs an explicit init (e.g. fixed).
  • kind = "WrappedBrownian" (alias "wb"): Brownian motion on the circle, the process to use for gain phases — its wrapped-normal transitions make the prior exactly 2π-periodic, so the 2π-shifted modes a real-line phase prior produces are all equivalent. tau is the phase coherence time in hours — the gap over which the visibility-domain coherence E[exp(iΔθ)] falls by 1/e — in the same units as the OrnsteinUhlenbeck tau. Again either a number or a distribution spec.
  • kind = "WrappedOrnsteinUhlenbeck" (alias "wou"): the mean-reverting process on the circle (shortest-arc drift), for a phase pinned near a level — e.g. a gain-ratio phase drifting about a separate offset term. sigma is the circular marginal spread in radians, tau the reversion time in hours, mu an optional fixed circular mean. Centered coordinates only.
  • kind = "VonMisesProcess" (alias "vm"): the same mean-reverting circular process with the shortest-arc drift replaced by the smooth sine drift, which additionally supports the non-centered (whitened) coordinates — Comrade's default for it. Same sigma/tau/mu as WrappedOrnsteinUhlenbeck, and a drop-in replacement for it in its σ ≪ π regime of validity.

Throws on an unknown process name or a missing hyperparameter.

BlackboxComrade.reactant_opt — Method
reactant_opt(post::VLBIPosterior, optimiser; initial_params=nothing, maxiters=10_000,
             ntrials=1, log_stride=250, rng=Random.default_rng(),
             held=Dict{Symbol, Float64}(), space=nothing) -> (xopt, sol)

Optimize post on the Reactant device with an Optimisers.jl optimiser (e.g. Optimisers.Adam(η)). Mirrors comrade_opt/best_image: with no initial_params, runs ntrials random-restart optimizations (from independent prior draws) and keeps the one with the best objective — this is what avoids the bad local minima that a single start falls into. A supplied initial_params (e.g. a later tempering stage) is a single warm start. Returns the optimum xopt as a host NamedTuple and sol = (; objective = -logdensity). The sky parameters in held (name => value) stay at their values throughout. The optimization runs in the latent space space (nothing is the flat space; see Comrade.maybe_transport).

The posterior is moved to the device internally via prepare_device; the per-step program (value + Enzyme gradient + update) is compiled once and reused for every trial (the shapes are identical, so restarts are essentially free). Convergence is reported via @info every log_stride iterations; no per-iteration image checkpoints are written (rendering them on the host each step is far slower than the device step itself).

BlackboxComrade.retarder_jones — Method
retarder_jones(δ, ε) -> (w11, w21, w12, w22)

Jones matrix, in column-major order, of a retarder that converts linear feeds to circular: fast axis rotated by δ (radians) from its nominal 45° and retardance π/2 + ε. It is written in the circular basis and normalized so that it is the identity at δ = ε = 0:

W = [  cos(ε/2) - i a s e^{-iδ}    sin(ε/2) + i a s e^{iδ}
      -sin(ε/2) + i a s e^{-iδ}    cos(ε/2) + i a s e^{iδ}  ]

with a = cos(ε/2) + sin(ε/2) and s = sin δ; det W = 1. Its leakage W₁₂/W₁₁, W₂₁/W₂₂ satisfies D₂ = -conj(D₁), the two feeds staying orthogonal, and |D| = tan δ at ε = 0.

BlackboxComrade.sample_kinetic — Method
sample_kinetic(out, post, xopt, strategy, rng; starts=nothing, resume=false, inputs=nothing)
    -> Comrade.PosteriorSamples

strategy.chains chains of the KineticSamplers algorithm strategy.algorithm (MCLMC or WALNUTS) on post in the latent space of strategy.latent_space, compiled with Reactant, started at xopt jittered by 0.001 standard normals per latent coordinate, or chain k at starts[k] when starts is given. A PooledAdaptation of strategy.nadapt MCLMC steps or WALNUTS draws per chain, in blocks of strategy.chunk_size MCLMC steps or of KineticSamplers' default length for WALNUTS, adapts one step size and one low-rank inverse mass with strategy.stiff_directions stiff directions, each estimate from strategy.stiff_draws draws, for all chains. The MCLMC step size follows its energy error and is capped at the step size for which a harmonic mode of the 0.99 quantile of the whitened curvature at positions of the warmup's records advances by the phase mclmc_cap_phase per step. The MCLMC warmup sets its trajectory length from the autocorrelation times of the rows strategy.trajectory_rows, by their strategy.trajectory_quantile quantile (by default the slowest row) or their mean, times strategy.trajectory_factor, moves it by strategy.refit_trajectory_step after each refit, runs strategy.window_iterations windows after the adaptation, and ends with a final phase of strategy.final steps per chain. MCLMC warms up without step halving, except with integrator = "baoab", whose warmup halves steps as its sampling does. Its history and parameters go to out/mclmc_warmup.jls or out/walnuts_warmup.jls. While it runs, out/warmup holds a WarmupRecord: each chain's log density after every block in out/warmup/trace.jls, rewritten at each logged block (every 50 draws' worth of blocks), and its position at each logged block as a draw of out/warmup/chain_k. After a final phase, a warning names the scalar parameters that still trend in it (see warn_trends). With strategy.trajectory_length, the MCLMC warmup runs no window and the trajectory length is strategy.trajectory_length times √d, and with strategy.step_size MCLMC samples at that step size instead of the warmup's. After the MCLMC warmup, KineticSamplers.measure_bias estimates the bias of the sampling parameters from coupled runs of strategy.bias_steps steps with one and two substeps (see bias_check), whose tolerances are the Monte Carlo errors at an effective sample size of 100; strategy.bias_steps = 0 skips it. With strategy.bias_tuning, KineticSamplers.tune_step_size reduces the step size until the estimates are within them, in at most 10 rounds of coupled runs of up to 16 times strategy.bias_steps steps and at most 0.25 of the sampling's gradients (see bias_budget), logging each round, and the warmup file holds the reduced step size. Without it the estimate is logged, with a warning if some estimate exceeds its tolerance by more than its margin of standard errors, and sampling continues at the warmup's step size. Then 10 blocks at the sampling parameters follow, whose energy statistics are logged per chain; if they fail KineticSamplers.check_energy, a warning says so and sampling continues.

Every strategy.checkpoint_minutes minutes of wall time (none for 0), at the end of a block, the warmup writes a checkpoint to out/mclmc_warmup_checkpoint.jls or out/walnuts_warmup_checkpoint.jls, replacing the one before: KineticSamplers' warmup state (see KineticSamplers.WarmupCheckpoint), the WarmupRecord, the scalar parameters of the final phase that warn_trends checks, the state of rng and of the moves, whose step scales adapt in the warmup, the seeds of the sampling generators, the elapsed time, and inputs, the SHA-256 digests of the run's model inputs, the image, instrument and data configs and the data and array files (see input_digests), or nothing. Each checkpoint is written to a file with the suffix .tmp and then renamed, so the checkpoint file always holds a whole checkpoint; a .tmp file left by a run that stopped while writing one is an error at the next start. The checkpoint is removed once the warmup file is written.

With resume, MCLMC sampling whose warmup finished (out/mclmc_warmup.jls) and which wrote draws continues instead: no warmup, the parameters of that file, each chain from its last draw, appending draws until strategy.nsample, without the bias check or tuning; the trajectory report then covers all draws. With resume, a warmup checkpoint and no warmup file, the warmup continues from the checkpoint, with out/warmup cut back to it, and the run then goes on as one that was not interrupted: the same warmup, bias check and draws, given the same settings. It throws if the checkpoint's latent dimension, chains, sampler, moves, warmup settings, input digests or rng type differ from the run's, before it changes out/warmup, rng or the moves. Otherwise, without draws the warmup starts afresh, which needs out/warmup and any checkpoint moved away.

Sampling keeps strategy.nsample draws per chain, chain k written as a Comrade sample directory out/chain_k: for MCLMC the last position of every strategy.thin-th block, so the chains run strategy.nsample * strategy.thin blocks and each kept draw's num_grads, time, divergent and halved count all the blocks since the previous kept draw; every draw for WALNUTS (WALNUTS(; max_depth = strategy.max_tree_depth)). The moves of strategy run on every chain in every phase of the warmup and in sampling, every 50 blocks for MCLMC and every 10 blocks for WALNUTS; a chain that moved restarts at its new position. Returns about 500 draws, evenly thinned from each chain, as a draws × chains PosteriorSamples; the chains on disk keep every draw (see load_kinetic_chains). After MCLMC sampling, trajectory_report logs the autocorrelation times of the scalar parameters and writes them to out/trajectory_report.toml.

BlackboxComrade.unwrap_phase_chains — Method
unwrap_phase_chains(post, x) -> (x′, nchanged)

The parameters x of post with every real-line Gauss–Markov phase chain (see PHASE_CHAIN_TERMS) rebuilt, within each site's path, as the running sum of its steps wrapped to [−π, π]: the path takes the shortest step between consecutive points, and the sum restarts at each point post fixes, which keeps its value. Only multiples of 2π change, so the likelihood is unchanged. nchanged counts the free points that moved. For real-line chains only (the StdNormal space); a wrapped chain stores angles on [−π, π].

BlackboxComrade.warn_trends — Method
warn_trends(samples; threshold = 1)

Warns about the scalar parameters that trend over samples, a vector over time of the scalar_leaves of every chain: those whose mean moves between the first and second half of the samples in the same direction in every chain, by more than threshold times the standard deviation within a half, pooled over chains and halves. Needs at least four samples and two chains, and warns that it cannot check otherwise.

BlackboxComrade.write_caltables — Method
write_caltables(out, post, xopt)

Write one CSV (and PNG via the plotting hook) calibration table per instrument quantity in xopt.instrument. With a self-calibrated station phase, a further table selfcal_phase holds the station phase itself: the self-calibration solution plus the fitted residual. No-op if xopt has no instrument component.
